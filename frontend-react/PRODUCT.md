# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- Children of about 5 to 8 years who are starting or strengthening reading and writing (reference case: a 7-year-old girl who recognizes the vowels and needs support to join them with consonants, sounds, syllables and words). They play on a tablet, usually next to an adult.
- Tutors: the parent, mother or responsible adult in Colombia who creates the child's profile, sets the session time and reads the activity record.
- Teachers (later increment): use AstroVet as classroom support with a class room where each child plays with their own avatar.
- On the public landing the visitor is usually the adult, on a computer or phone, sometimes with the child beside them.

## Product Purpose

Support early reading practice through short gamified activities with visual and spoken instructions, and let tutors and teachers see descriptive records of what was practiced. Success: the child wants to come back, practices letters, sounds, syllables and first words, and the adult understands what happened in each session.

## Positioning

Learning to read is a rescue mission: each mission helps a lost animal, guided by Capitán Misi, an astronaut cat veterinarian. Vowels stay present the whole route; consonants arrive one at a time by their sound, never their name.

## Operating Context

- Sessions of 5 to 10 minutes configured by the tutor, with a warning one minute before and a calm closing scene.
- Three MVP worlds: Planeta Nebulosa (vowels), Luna Mágica (M, P, S, L and syllables), Selva Cósmica (first words). Later worlds extend the route.
- The public landing should let the visitor play first and then invite them to create an account.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Errors are never punished: no lost paws, lives or progress. Every error gets feedback; after several consecutive errors the game gives hints; if the child still cannot pass, Misi helps complete it and explains.
- One instruction at a time, repeatable audio, large touch targets (minimum 48 × 48 px), moderate stimuli that can be reduced or muted.
- Games within a world should vary so the child does not repeat the same game mission after mission.
- AstroVet does not diagnose ADHD or any condition and does not replace professional assessment. Reports are descriptive.
- Children's data follows Colombian Law 1581 of 2012; profiles use nicknames, not full names.
- Stack in place: React + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind CSS; backend planned in FastAPI + PostgreSQL.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: AstroVet. Tagline: «Jugando hoy, leyendo mañana».
- Character: Capitán Misi, astronaut cat with veterinary elements; rescued animals.
- Palette from the product document: lavender #8E7CC3, pastel pink #FFB7B2, sky blue #E8F0FE, gold #FFD166.
- Voice: short sentences, tuteo, praise effort not intelligence; never «te equivocaste» as reproach, «rápido», «perdiste» or comparisons between children.
- All published illustrations, audio and sounds must be original or commercially licensed.

## Evidence on Hand
